Experiment guide

Student lab
1. Build: Add hosts, routers, and switches. Connect nodes and inspect their IP-less logical topology.
2. Configure: Change link bandwidth, propagation delay, loss, and queue capacity.
3. Generate: Create UDP-like flows between hosts, and watch packet events.
4. Diagnose: Inject congestion or disable a link; observe route changes and drops.
5. Compare: Export CSV traces and JSON topologies to reproduce experiments.
Datagram-only teaching model: explicit per-device next-hop tables, Dijkstra/Bellman–Ford and node-defined packet rules. No real IP, MAC learning, TCP, ARP or wireless PHY. Hosts do not forward transit traffic.
